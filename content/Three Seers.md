The **Three Seers** are three notable people known in [[Helixism]] as warrior demigods who double as oracles, each capable of guiding fate with their [[Voideye|eyes]] and [[Blessing of Creation|blessings.]]

The [[Helixite Orthodoxy|Helixite orthodox churches]] claim them to be prophets that guide the [[The Meridian|Meridian]] through the [[Ages|Ages,]] preventing the world from succumbing to [[Calamity|Calamity.]]

Helixite priests state that only three Seers can live at the same time; when one Seer dies, the next person born on the [[The Meridian|Meridian]] will be take up the power. Thus, the title of Seer moves from person to person, and not all Seers are historically notable. However, given their unique affinity for magic, many Seers have been known to live many times their species' average lifespan.

# Current Three Seers
---
## Bethaby, Queen of Blades
##### "Slayer of Calamities," "Devil of the Prairielands"

* As the first of the current [[Three Seers]] of [[Helixism|Helixism,]] Saint Bethaby is the most powerful member of the [[Church of Chains]] and a vassal of the [[Lord of Chains|Lord of Chains.]] She is a living legend, a half-fiend warrior of overwhelming power who has lived since the [[Age of Rime|third Age]] and slain all manner of unholy terrors. She is the generally known to be the oldest living Seer on the [[The Meridian|Meridian.]] She is known for her incredible strength, martial genius, and transcendent swordsmanship, which has earned her title Queen of Blades. 
## Neska, Landshaper
#####  "Witch of Time," "Hammer of the One Mother"
* As one of the current Three Seers of Helixism, Saint Neska is a high matriarch in the [[Church of the Mother Tree|Church of the Mother Tree.]] She is the youngest of the Seers, but no less cunning or wise. She is known for her ingenuity and craftsmanship, and is known to be ruthless and loving in equal measure. While not the premiere warrior of the Seers, Neska is still extremely powerful: her use of magic, combined with her seer abilities and tactical experience, allow her to bend entire battlefields to her will.
## Altan, Wandering Wind
* As one of the current Three Seers of Helixism, Saint Altan makes allegiance with no specific Helixite church, acting instead as a wandering beneficiary and mission for the greater [[Helixite Orthodoxy|Helixite Orthodoxy.]] He is the least publicly known Seer, preferring to act and travel alone across the land. He is known to be a frighteningly swift fighter, one capable of sculpting the wind into devastating elemental attacks.
