The **Khanate of Xetrivaar,** known coloquially as **Xetrivaar,** is a nation located within the prairielands of central [[Altinin|Altinin.]] Xetrivaar is known for their many tribal and nomadic cultures that wander the prairie, united under a central chiefdom lead by their **Khan.** Xetrivaar is known for being the seat of power of the [[Church of Chains|Church of Chains]] and their patron saint, the warrior [[Seer]] Bethaby. The capital of Xetrivaar is [[Vaari|Vaari.]] Folk from Xetrivaar, as well as the tribes they comprise, are known as *Xetri.*

# History
Shortly after the [[Great Fusion|first Calamity,]] many fledgling nomadic cultures began to take shape in what would eventually become the Chainlands, or prairielands, of central Altinin—known for the terrifying beasts that roamed them. These early tribes mastered a form of magic beast-taming that allowed them to train, and eventually domesticate, the worst of the beasts. To this day, however, the ravenous prairie dogs that emerge at night have yet to be fully conquered. Their presence is diminished near the capital [[Vaari]], where the khan rules.

The many tribes of Xetrivaar would only come into true relevance with the appearance of the [[Godchain]] and emergence of the [[Church of Chains]] at the beginning of the [[Age of Rime|third Age.]] With the will of the [[Lord of Chains]]  and the greater [[Helixite Orthodoxy]] cast over the prairie, they became known as the Chainlands accordingly. The khan's power and influence became nearly divine, shifting the dynamic between Xetrivaar and its neighbors greatly.

Xetrivaar is also known for its participation in the [[Thousand Days' War|Thousand Days' War,]] namely the use of the Chainlands as a continuous battleground against the forces of humankind during the latter half of the war. 

# Politics
Xetrivaar is defined by the many colorful Xetri tribes that unite under the rule of the [[Vaarinan Khan|Vaarinan Khan,]] the absolute leader of the greater khanate. 

The numerous tribes under the khan are each led by a [[Chainlord|chainlord,]] who are each a vassal of the khan and thus an extension of his will. Chainlords are trained from birth to be warriors of unmatched strength, intelligence, and wisdom, and lead the Xetri tribes till death.

Each tribe is distinct, but many are nomadic in some way, preferring to travel across the prairie throughout the year. No one tribe owns any particular region on paper, but in practice, the most dominant tribes often wander the least dangerous expanses of the prairielands. Ultimately, all tribes serve the Khan, and may be called to the capital to serve him for one reason or another.

# Culture & Religion
Xetri people are known for their steadfast nature, unbreakable wills, and strong social bonds. The doctrine of the [[Church of Chains]] guides Xetri society—which was already a warrior culture—to become solely dictated by merit, particularly when it comes to tactical intelligence, social maturity, and pure martial prowess. 

On the prairie, only the strong survive: a truth all who walk among its grasses are forced to acknowledge. To the Xetri, fear is not an option. All conflicts, misfortunes,  and setbacks are trials, and it is only by confronting these challenges head-on that one can hope to not just survive, but prevail with confidence, adding a link to their chain and forging themselves stronger.

The Xetri place a strong emphasis on bonds of loyalty and trust, the necessary burden of responsibility, and the opportunities that arise with hardship, concepts which are each reflected by the teachings and doctrines of their Church:

At birth, all people are "linked" to the land, where they must toil in life to earn the right to become a part of the Primordial Chain. Under the watchful eye of their Lord, every man must try his hardest to become a paragon of strength, wisdom, and leadership, challenging every trial he faces with the grim determination of a warrior. It is only by treating every setback as an opportunity for growth, taking responsibility for one's situation, and honoring one's bonds that one can earn the right to be hoisted to their Lord's heaven. 

Heretics—those who disrespect an especially important bond, break a national contract, or abandon the Church—are branded as chainbreakers, and are cast out of Xetri society.
