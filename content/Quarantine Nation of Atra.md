The **Quarantine Nation of Atra,** known coloquially as **Atra,** is a state almost entirely undisturbed by other nations across [[Altinin]] due to the ever-present threat of the [[Scarabs|scarabs]] that roam the sterile [[Crystal Barrens|Crystal Barrens.]] Atra is the sole exporter of [[Ocrys|ocrys,]] which is grown near its capital city [[Delta|Delta.]] Atra recently became a vassal state of the [[High Empire of Avalan|Avalanian Empire.]]People from Atra are known as *Atrans.*
